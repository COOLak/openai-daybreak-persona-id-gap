# Timeline

Times are **UTC**. Support-chat times are approximate, taken from the local clock at the time of the conversation.

## Before November 4, 2026: verification blocked

After Advanced Account Security and two YubiKeys from the OpenAI + Yubico bundle were registered, the Daybreak identity-verification step opened. With **Russia** selected, Persona showed **"We're unable to verify IDs from this country."** With **Japan** selected, Persona offered only a driver's license or passport, with no residence card option.

## November 4, 2026, about 06:30 UTC: support request

A request through the OpenAI Help Center support chat asked OpenAI to accept the Japanese Residence Card or allow manual review, or else to state whether nationality makes the account ineligible.

## November 4, 2026, about 06:31 UTC: support assistant reply

The AI support assistant replied that Daybreak "document types can include residence permits", but that Support "can't override the verification decision or force a manual review", and that Daybreak "isn't available in every region". It asked which country had been selected.

## November 4, 2026, about 06:36 UTC: follow-up

The reply explained both paths (Russia rejected; Japan offers only a driver's license or passport). It asked how to verify with the Residence Card, or for confirmation of whether nationality is the reason.

## November 4, 2026, about 06:38 UTC: escalated

The assistant escalated the conversation to a support specialist: "You can expect a response in the coming days. Replies will also be sent via email."
