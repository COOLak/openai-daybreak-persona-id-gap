OpenAI Daybreak incident · November 4, 2026

Timeline

Times are UTC. Support-chat times are approximate, taken from the local clock at the time of the conversation.

Before November 4, 2026: verification blocked

After Advanced Account Security and two YubiKeys from the OpenAI + Yubico bundle were registered, identity verification opened. With Russia selected, Persona showed “We're unable to verify IDs from this country.” With Japan selected, it offered only a driver's license or passport.

November 4, about 06:30 UTC: support request

A request through the Help Center support chat asked OpenAI to accept the Japanese Residence Card or allow manual review, or else to state whether nationality makes the account ineligible.

About 06:31 UTC: support assistant reply

Daybreak “document types can include residence permits,” but Support “can't override the verification decision or force a manual review,” and Daybreak “isn't available in every region.”

About 06:36 UTC: follow-up

The reply explained both Persona paths and asked how to verify with the Residence Card, or for confirmation of whether nationality is the reason.

About 06:38 UTC: escalated

Escalated to a support specialist: “You can expect a response in the coming days. Replies will also be sent via email.”

Current status

Unresolved. No human response, rule citation or alternative verification path has been provided.
